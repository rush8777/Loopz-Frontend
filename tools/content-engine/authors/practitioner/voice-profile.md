# Movcues house voice: Hands-on product practitioner

Status: **starter hypothesis**; refine after 20 permitted references are collected. This is an original editorial style, not an imitation or byline of the source writer.

## Voice principles

- Tone: Practical, stepwise, precise
- Editorial structure: Start with the job-to-be-done, show setup/diagnostics, pitfalls, examples, and checklist.
- Opening: Start with a recognizable decision, workflow, or problem in plain language. Show its practical consequence and promise a useful next step; do not invent first-hand experience.
- Accessibility: Explain necessary technical terms when first used, with a brief example. Translate diagnostics into questions and actions a nontechnical reader can follow without sacrificing accuracy.
- Claim discipline: state what's observed, what is inferred, what is hypothetical, and the source.
- Avoid: copied wording, signatures/pet phrases from identifiable writers, stock intros, unsupported statistics and unnatural keyword repetition.
- Paragraphs: vary deliberately for reading flow; headings should describe reader value.

## Training protocol

1. Collect 20 permitted articles from one selected reference author, one at a time. Fill `references/manifest.csv` with URL, canonical, date, permission, topic and independently verified performance evidence.
2. Keep local reference text outside Git. Do not publish or commit scraped copies.
3. Use 15 for analysis and hold 5 out for evaluation. Extract distributions: sentence length, paragraph length, hook type, rhetorical structure, concrete-example frequency, evidence density, heading conventions, audience assumptions and conclusions.
4. Distill broad transferable characteristics while avoiding idiosyncratic language and signature phrases.
5. Evaluate original Movcues sample drafts against profile, usefulness and originality. Record independent reviewer notes and revise this profile.
6. Profile byline must be a real approved contributor, not the reference author.

## Evaluation rubric

| Dimension | Pass criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Originality | Distinct wording and original idea/example |
| Voice | Consistent with broad intended tone, without imitation |
| Clarity | Reader can act on the explanation |
| Accuracy | All material factual claims grounded |
| Brand | Supports Movcues audience without forced sales pitch |
