# Non-negotiable content and operational policies

1. **Do not copy references.** Reference articles supply broad editorial observations and hypotheses about search intent; final prose, examples, diagrams, and claims must be original. No long verbatim reuse or close paraphrases. Do not claim to be an individual writer whose work was sampled.
2. **Separate performance evidence.** Collect dated ranking screenshots/exported analytics only where authorized. Article success is not established by how well-written it looks or how often shared.
3. **No hallucinated metrics.** If no search volume, difficulty, traffic, authority, citations or conversion evidence is available, say `unknown`; no made-up numbers.
4. **Verify all material claims.** Cite primary sources when available. Record exact pages and dates. Review statistics for correct denominator/sample/time period; reject unsupported figures.
5. **Product truth.** Verify features, plans, performance, integrations and pricing against current Movcues code/docs or explicitly approved source of truth, not old marketing copy.
6. **AEO / GEO.** Direct answer where appropriate; accurate headings, named entities, citations and well-structured HTML. No 'AI magic schema', `llms.txt` ranking guarantees, fabricated quotes, or keyword stuffing. Search engine features and AI citations must be measured as external outcomes.
7. **SEO.** Satisfy actual user intent. Avoid unnecessary duplicate posts, artificial word count, forced exact match density, automated link spam, or fake EEAT credentials.
8. **Privacy and copyright.** Respect source site restrictions. Store full fetched reference text outside Git and prevent it from being sent into deploy artifacts. Store citation URLs/metadata in tracked manifest; personal data only with authorization.
9. **Human sign-off.** Never self-generate an approval on the user's behalf. Require affirmative human action for publication, tied to a fingerprint and verified CMS draft. A manual approval file by itself is only a workflow record, not a secure authorization boundary.
10. **Non-destructive integration.** Do not change main directly, delete existing docs, weaken authentication, expose Sanity secrets, publish test content, or overwrite uncommitted local code.
11. **Bylines.** An editorial or fictional byline may be used if clearly disclosed to readers; do not invent a biography, credentials, or first-hand experience. Obtain a real person's permission before attributing an article to them. No personal approval is needed for a fictional persona.
