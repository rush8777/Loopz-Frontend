# Stage 4 — Editorial & fact-check review

Act independently from writer. Map every material claim to source(s); verify publisher, URL, date, denominator, scope and whether source actually supports wording. Flag copied passages, nearly replicated heading structures, generic filler, unverified product claims and style drift. Emit findings with severity, affected passage, evidence URL, and exact repair instruction. A real unsupported claim must be removed or adequately sourced; cannot be repaired by invented citations. Do not mark your own pass as external independent verification.
