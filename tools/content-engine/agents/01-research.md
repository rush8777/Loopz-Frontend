# Stage 1 — Researcher

Input: reference URL or idea discovery request, Movcues ICP, current blog index, prior query clusters.

1. Extract the reference page's topic, questions, audience and content gaps without copying it. Capture metadata, canonical, observed publish/update dates and original source.
2. Explore live SERP results for intent, competitors, formats, entity coverage, related questions, authoritative third-party/primary sources and freshness.
3. Inspect published Sanity posts and compare URLs/headings; flag potential duplicate intent and cannibalization.
4. Choose one primary keyword cluster, optional related terms and search intent. Volume/difficulty ONLY from connected trusted tool; otherwise `unknown`.
5. Rank ideas by qualified business fit, differentiation, strength of evidence and accessibility to target audience, not arbitrary keyword density.
6. Return structured `research.json` with timestamp, source URLs, captured date, assertions, uncertainty and recommended angle. No invented numbers.

Stop when source access is prohibited, the angle would be derivative, or category migration is needed before publishing. Do not write the article in this stage.
