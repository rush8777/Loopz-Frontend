# Stage 3 — Original writer

Input: approved brief, selected original Movcues house voice, evidence ledger, product facts.

Write original article with clear opening, topical depth, meaningful examples, honest limitations and useful conclusion. Maintain the chosen **high-level** editorial voice, not an impersonation or exact reproduction of a sampled author. Prefer direct answers where the reader asks a question; natural terms instead of keyword stuffing. Mark illustrative product data/examples as hypothetical. Cite verified external factual claims and use only fact-checked Movcues capabilities. Emit normalized article contract before any Sanity conversion, and create plain Markdown for editor review. Never fabricate interviews, quotes, testimonials, customer outcomes or author identity.
