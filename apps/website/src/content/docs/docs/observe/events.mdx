**Observe → Events** lists the custom actions your product sends to Movcues. An event such as `report_exported` can represent a successful outcome. Automatic clicks and page views are different signals; they do not become named product events in this list.

## Example

Your team wants to understand report exports. First ask a developer to send `report_exported` after a successful export, with useful non-sensitive details such as the file format. Then inspect who uses it and where it happens.

## Explore an event

1. Open **Observe → Events** for the correct site.
2. Use **Search events…** to find the exact event name.
3. Use **Filter** to choose a **Date range** or **Segment**. Sort by **Occurrences**, **Unique users**, **Sessions**, or the other available choices.
4. Open the event to inspect its details and date range.
5. Browse **Properties**, **Occurrences**, **Users**, **Sessions**, and **Pages**.

**Occurrences** counts each recorded instance. **Unique users** counts distinct identities; **Sessions** counts visits containing the event. Ten exports by one user are not ten users.

## Inspect the details

**Properties** summarizes the details attached to the event, such as `format`. An occurrence opens its timestamp, page context, and supplied values. From there, open **View session** or the available user/visitor profile link to understand its context.

A missing property means it may not have been sent, or may not exist on the selected occurrences. It is not a field you can fill in for past events from this screen.
