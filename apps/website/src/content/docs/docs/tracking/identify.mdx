---
title: Identify users
description: Connect product activity to a signed-in account using a safe internal ID.
---

Before someone signs in, Movcues can record their activity under an anonymous browser ID. After sign-in, your app can tell Movcues which internal account is using the browser. This helps a product team understand activity by account without sending a person’s email or name.

## Example: understand a trial user’s journey

Your team wants to see whether trial users create a project after signing in. The app can send its own account ID and a small amount of useful context, such as the plan:

```js
window.analytics.identify("usr_123", {
  plan: "growth",
  workspace_id: "ws_456",
});
```

Here, `usr_123` is an example of an internal ID from your product. Your developer connects the call to the point where sign-in succeeds.

## When to use it

- After a user signs in or creates an account.
- After the user switches to a different account.
- Before recording the next important action as a signed-in user.

Movcues keeps the identity for the active browser session. Activity before sign-in starts under an anonymous browser ID; do not assume it is automatically attached to a named account. The SDK’s anonymous ID allows activity to be recorded before login.

## Clear identity when the user signs out

```js
window.analytics.reset();
```

Your developer should call `reset()` on sign-out or before another person uses the same browser. This prevents activity from the next session from being attributed to the previous account.

## Keep account details limited

The optional details can help answer questions such as whether people on a trial or paid plan use a feature. Keep them limited to current, product-related information your team is allowed to collect:

```js
window.analytics.identify(user.id, {
  account_role: user.role,
  billing_plan: user.plan,
});
```

Do not use `identify()` to upload a full customer record. Avoid email addresses, names, payment details, and other sensitive information.
