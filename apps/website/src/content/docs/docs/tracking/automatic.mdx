---
title: Automatic collection
description: Learn what Movcues records for you and what those signals can tell your product team.
---

Automatic collection means Movcues records some product activity as soon as the SDK is installed. Your team can get an initial picture of how people move through the product before a developer adds custom events.

## What Movcues records

| Activity | What it can help you understand | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page views | Which pages or app screens people visit. | See whether new users reach the project setup screen after signing up. |
| Interactive clicks | Which buttons, links, or other controls people use. | See whether visitors choose “Create project” or leave the page. |
| Scroll progress | How far people move down a page. Movcues records milestones such as 25%, 50%, and 75%. | Check whether people reach the plan comparison or setup instructions. |
| Repeated clicks | When someone clicks the same area several times in a short period. This can be a clue that a control is confusing or unresponsive. | Look for repeated clicks on “Invite teammate” when users try to finish setup. |

These signals show what happened in the interface; they do not explain a user’s intention or prove that a task succeeded. For outcomes such as “project created” or “invite accepted,” add a [custom event](/docs/tracking/custom-events/).

## Turn off automatic tracking

If a signal is not useful to your team, ask your developer to switch off automatic clicks, scroll progress, or repeated-click tracking in the SDK settings. For example, a team focused on button use may not need scroll progress. The [Configuration guide](/docs/configuration/) explains the settings. For sensitive areas, use [privacy controls](/docs/privacy/).
