---
title: "Troubleshoot an experience that does not appear"
description: "Check publication, audience, page, trigger, timing, frequency, and targets in a deliberate order."
---

A preview can render even when a normal visitor does not qualify for delivery. Test the published experience outside the live editor using an account and page that should match.

## Check in order

1. **Publication:** confirm the experience is **Live**, not Draft or Paused. If you edited it, publish the revised draft through **Review & publish**.
2. **Connection:** select the right site and run [the live SDK test](/docs/getting-started/connect/). The product must use this site’s snippet and configured domain.
3. **Audience:** open the saved Segment and confirm the test visitor is a current member. Check exclusions in combined rules.
4. **Page:** confirm the normal product URL matches the experience’s saved page rules. Review **Allowed origins** if configured.
5. **Trigger:** reach the page, perform a fresh selected custom event, or use the configured explicit launch action. A manual trigger does not display automatically.
6. **Schedule:** check start/end times and whether the active window has passed.
7. **Frequency:** the test user may already have seen it. Check **Only once**, session limits, cooldown, and maximum impressions.
8. **Target:** for an anchored step or hotspot, open the live editor and confirm the intended element is **Found on current page** in the relevant user state.
9. **Competing experiences:** a current guide or higher-priority eligible experience may defer another. Check orchestration rather than adding more launches.

## Publishing is blocked

Read **Needs attention** in the review. Fix missing targets, invalid Segment/Page references, incomplete survey questions, and unavailable linked Guides. A broad automatic launch requires its explicit acknowledgement. Check role and plan limits if the server rejects an otherwise valid configuration.

## Survey is visible but has no responses

Check that the final button uses **Survey: Submit**, that required questions can be answered, and that you are reading the correct Results date range. A showing or an in-progress answer is not a submitted response.

## Checklist is missing or a task stays incomplete

Check audience/page eligibility and whether that identity permanently dismissed or acknowledged completion. Verify each task’s **Complete when** rule. Opening a destination does not satisfy a Segment-based completion rule until the required behavior is recorded. A **Launch Guide** action needs a published, available Guide.

If the editor itself will not open, allow popups and launch a fresh session on the configured product origin. Keep the visible error and reproduction steps for support; do not share the editor-session URL.
