Use the dashboard canvas to edit your message, then the live editor to check it on your product. Previewing a draft is different from proving that normal visitors meet its published targeting rules.

## Before opening

The site needs a configured **Primary domain**, the SDK installed on that product, and a build URL on the same origin. Sign in to your product if the target is behind login. Allow the live-editor popup for Movcues.

## Use the live editor

1. Open the draft and choose **Open live editor**. On a small dashboard screen, the button is labeled **Preview**.
2. Wait for **Movcues Live Editor** and its connected status on your real product.
3. Use **Navigate** to interact normally with the product and open the right screen or menu.
4. Use **Select** to choose an element for an anchored card, hotspot, or anchored guide step. Use **Reselect target** to change it.
5. Check **Live status**, including **Found on current page** or the missing-target message.
6. Review **Placement** and the visible preview, then wait for **Draft saved**.
7. For a guide or survey, select each numbered step and inspect it on its intended page.

Choose a stable, visible product control. A temporary loading placeholder or an element available only to an administrator may not exist for your audience. Ask a developer for a stable target attribute if selection is fragile.

## Layering

**Automatic** is the starting policy. **Relative to an element** lets you place guidance above or below a selected element’s layer. **Always on top** and **Advanced / Custom** are available for specific cases; check that the result does not cover navigation or close controls.

## Preview check and delivery check

| Check | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard canvas/device preview | Content fits the chosen editing viewport. |
| Live editor | The draft can render in the actual product context and find its target. |
| Normal visit after publishing | A real visitor meets audience, page, trigger, timing, and frequency rules. |

Checklists use their own canvas **Progress** and **View** preview controls; they do not open in this live editor.

If the connection expires, close it and launch a fresh editor session from the dashboard. Never share its editor-session URL.
