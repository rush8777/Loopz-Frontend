---
title: "Save, review, and publish"
description: "Review the actual launch conditions, publish a saved draft, and manage later revisions."
---

Saving a draft preserves your work. Publishing makes a saved version available to eligible visitors. Use the review to check the intended audience and moment before launching.

## Before publishing

Use an Owner or Admin account. Wait for **Saved**, verify any anchored targets, and confirm your plan supports the selected settings. A publish error may concern permissions, capacity, targeting, or content rather than design.

## Complete the launch review

1. Choose **Review & publish** in the editor.
2. On first launch, follow **Launch setup** using **Continue**:
   - Choose **Who should see this experience?**
   - Choose **Where should this experience appear?**
   - For guides/widgets, choose **What should make this experience appear?**
   - Choose frequency and the optional active window.
   - Review the guide steps, survey questions, checklist tasks, or widget behavior.
3. On **Review and publish**, read the **Launch summary** and each condition. Use its **Edit** action to correct it.
4. Resolve every item in **Needs attention**. Review warnings too.
5. If an automatic experience can appear to everyone across all pages, confirm **I intend to show this experience this broadly.** only when that is deliberate.
6. Choose **Publish now** and wait for **Published**.

Returning experiences open on the review summary. Checklists omit the trigger and repeat-frequency stages because they use persistent task state.

## Verify the launch

Use a normal product visit with a qualifying test user, outside the live editor. Check appearance and actions. Then review [experience performance](/docs/analytics/overview/). **Published** confirms the saved release, not that everyone will see it immediately.

## Edit a live experience

Open **Edit** and make changes. Autosave updates the draft; the current live version continues until you publish the revised draft. Reopen **Review & publish** and recheck targeting as well as visuals.

Use **Pause** from the collection when you need delivery stopped. A later **Publish** makes the experience available again if the configuration remains valid; it is not a fresh-user reset for frequency or checklist progress.
