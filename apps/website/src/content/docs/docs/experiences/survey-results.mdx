---
title: "Read and investigate survey responses"
description: "Review survey participation, inspect submissions, and turn a structured answer group into a segment."
---

Open **Experiences → Surveys → View Results** for a survey. Its **Overview**, **Responses**, and **Investigate** sections answer different questions. Use the **Date range** to include the period you want to study.

## Overview: what happened?

**Seen** counts unique people shown the survey. **Responses** counts submitted response records. **Response rate** is unique respondents divided by people seen. **Abandonment** describes started responses not submitted. Completion time describes the interval from start to submission.

The overview also shows question summaries, **Most common responses**, **Latest feedback**, and **Areas worth investigating** when data is available. Multiple-choice percentages can total more than 100% because one person can choose several options. **Previous version** labels distinguish older question definitions.

## Responses: what did someone say?

1. Open **Responses**.
2. Narrow by response status, respondent type, and, for structured questions, question and answer.
3. Open the answer preview to read **Response details**.
4. Open the respondent’s profile link when available to inspect their product activity.

A status of **In progress** or **Abandoned** is different from a submitted response. Avoid treating partial answers as completed feedback.

## Investigate: who needs follow-up?

Example: users who rate export difficulty low may need guidance.

1. Open **Investigate** and select a **Question** and **Selected response**.
2. Review the observed group and **Recent respondents**.
3. Use **View respondents** to inspect records.
4. An Owner or Admin can choose **Create segment**, name it, and save it.

The segment keeps a rolling range based on the selected Results period. Choice questions match the selected answer, ratings match that value, and NPS answers create a promoter/passive/detractor category group. Check the saved definition: an NPS category can cover more than the single score you selected.

Use the saved Segment in an existing funnel’s **Analyze users** filter or [experience targeting](/docs/targeting/audience/). Direct response-to-event comparison is not available here.

A response group is evidence of an answer, not a diagnosis of its cause. Read individual feedback before deciding what to change.
