---
title: "Manage your plan and usage"
description: "Review subscription status and capacity, open Paddle billing, and understand the MAU allowance."
---

Open **Settings → Plan & Usage** to see the workspace’s subscription, trial information, monthly active users, and resource capacity. Use the displayed limits as the current allowance for your workspace.

## Review capacity

The page shows **Sites**, **Members**, **Dashboards**, **Segments**, **Funnels**, and **Published experiences**. These are different from the number of users visiting your product.

Example: an unused pending invitation can still occupy a team seat. Revoke it in [Team settings](/docs/workspace/team/) if it should no longer be valid. If a resource limit prevents creation, remove an unused resource where appropriate or choose a plan with more capacity.

## Choose or manage a subscription

Owners and Admins can use the billing actions when billing is configured.

1. Open **Settings → Plan & Usage**.
2. For an eligible trial, expired, or canceled subscription, choose **Choose Starter**, **Choose Growth**, or **Choose Scale** to open Paddle checkout.
3. For an active subscription, choose **Manage billing** to open the Paddle customer portal for payment details, invoices, and available subscription changes.
4. After completing an action, return and reload **Plan & Usage** to confirm the subscription state.

If checkout fails to open, read the visible error and try once more after reloading. If the problem persists, contact support with the workspace ID and error text; do not send payment-card details. A checkout transaction being created is not proof of a completed subscription.

## What MAU means here

Billable monthly active users count distinct identified product users with recorded activity from each site’s configured production origin in a UTC calendar month. Anonymous visits alone do not add an identified MAU. This can differ from a broad analytics **Unique users** count.

The MAU allowance is a **soft cap**: crossing it prompts an upgrade request but does not stop collection or existing experience delivery. Other resource limits and advanced-feature access can still restrict creation or configuration.

Advanced audience combinations, event/manual launches, scheduling, frequency caps, guide progression, and orchestration are plan-dependent. If a change is blocked, check the available plan options rather than repeatedly republishing.
