A workspace holds your team and subscription. A site holds the product activity, saved audiences, funnels, and experiences for one connected product context.

## Select the right context

Open **Settings → Workspace**. If you belong to more than one workspace, choose **Current workspace**. The page shows **Workspace name**, **Workspace ID**, and **Your role**. Owners and Admins can rename the workspace and choose **Save**.

Open **Settings → Sites**, or click the site name at the top of the main sidebar. Select a site; **Current** marks the active choice. Close settings and verify that the dashboard now shows the intended site.

## Create or edit a site

Owners and Admins can:

1. Choose **Add site**.
2. Enter **Site name** and, when available, **Primary domain (optional)**.
3. Choose **Create site**. To deliver experiences and use domain-dependent features, configure the exact product origin, such as `https://app.example.com`.
4. For an existing site, choose **Rename** under **Selected site** to edit its name and **Primary domain**, then **Save changes**.

Example: marketing pages on `https://example.com` and the app on `https://app.example.com` are different origins. Use the site’s own snippet on the product it represents. See [Connect your site](/docs/getting-started/connect/).

## Delete carefully

Under **Danger zone**, **Delete site…** asks you to type the site name before choosing **Delete site**. This permanently removes the site and associated analytics data. It is different from deleting a Page definition or pausing an experience.

## Account and privacy information

**Settings → Account** shows your Name and Email and offers **Sign out**; it does not provide profile editing controls. **Data & Privacy** describes collection capabilities and is not a set of collection toggles. A developer must apply [SDK privacy controls](/docs/privacy/). **Developer** provides public Site ID/configuration for your integration.
