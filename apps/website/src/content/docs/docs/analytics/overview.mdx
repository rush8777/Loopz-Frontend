---
title: "Read experience performance"
description: "Understand the Experience Overview and the differences between viewers, engagement, and completion."
---

**Workspace → Overview** opens **Experience Overview**. It summarizes how people respond to your published experiences. Use custom **Dashboards** when you want broader product metrics.

## Example

You launched a guide and a survey during onboarding. Use Overview to see delivery and interaction, then open the survey’s results to learn what people said.

## Read the overview

1. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Last 90 days**.
2. Select **Viewers**, **Engagement**, or **Completion** above **Performance over time** to change the trend.
3. Find an experience in the table. Search by name or filter by type and status.
4. Open the experience name to return to its editor, or its results when it is a survey.

| Measurement | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Viewers | Unique viewers per experience. The summary adds experience counts, so someone who sees two experiences can contribute twice. |
| Engagement rate | Started/interacting viewers divided by viewers. |
| Completion | Guide completion, survey response rate, or checklist completion where available. It does not mean the user completed your business goal. |
| Goal conversion | No configured goal data is currently available here. |

The search, type, and status controls filter the experience table; the summary and trend still cover the selected date range across experiences.

## Check individual results

The experience collections also show compact performance summaries. A survey’s **View Results** opens [question summaries and individual responses](/docs/experiences/survey-results/). A [custom dashboard](/docs/analytics/dashboards/) can place an experience card alongside product events or a funnel.

If the numbers look empty, first check the date range, site, and [delivery conditions](/docs/help/experience-delivery/).
