Ask about a task after the person has actually completed it. This example asks how easy it was to export a report, rather than interrupting users as soon as they sign in.

## Prepare the inputs

Confirm `report_exported` is recorded after export success in [Events](/docs/observe/events/). Choose the site, product Page, and audience. You need Owner or Admin access and plan access to an event-triggered launch.

## Build a short survey

1. Open **Experiences → Surveys → Create survey**.
2. Add a **Rating** question such as “How easy was it to export your report?” Use a clearly explained 1–5 scale.
3. Add an optional **Long text** question asking what could be improved.
4. Check required-answer behavior and the **Survey: Submit** button action.
5. [Preview the survey](/docs/experiences/surveys/) through submission.

## Launch at the right moment

Choose **Review & publish → When something happens → report_exported**. Set a suitable Page and audience, then limit repeat exposure. Complete the review and test a fresh export as an eligible user outside the editor.

## Read and investigate

Open **View Results**. Use **Overview** to check response rate and **Responses** to read submissions. Under **Investigate**, choose a structured answer group, inspect respondents, and optionally **Create segment** for relevant follow-up.

The response Segment is dynamic. For NPS it uses the selected score’s category, not necessarily that one score; inspect the resulting definition. Text answers can inform a decision but are not an automatic generic condition in the Segment builder.

Follow up with guidance only when the feedback and activity support it. A low rating indicates reported difficulty; inspect the context before assigning a cause.
